Checked 28 Oct 2026. Each claim on the how-it-works diagrams, the managing-symptoms page, and the appointment sheet is listed with the source section that supports it. Paraphrases are from the pages opened that day. A claim that could not be tied to a section was not published.

## Appointment sheet

The checkboxes are NICE 1.3.1 (the six symptom or sign groups) and 1.3.2 (a first-degree relative). The diary columns follow 1.3.3. No extra symptom was added.

## Urgent care

WHO and NICE do not publish a "seek urgent care if" list for endometriosis. The NHS endometriosis page labels seeing a GP as non-urgent advice, and it names chest pain, shortness of breath, and coughing up blood as symptoms to tell a doctor. Those sentences are in the symptoms callout. There is no danger-coloured urgent box, because neither WHO, NICE, nor that NHS page states one.
