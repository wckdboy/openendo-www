# Explainer claim audit

Checked 28 Oct 2026. Each claim on the how-it-works diagrams, the managing-symptoms page, and the appointment sheet is listed with the source section that supports it. Paraphrases are from the pages opened that day. A claim that could not be tied to a section was not published.

The mechanisms diagram connects nodes with cited edges. Oestrogen to lesion growth is ESHRE (estrogen-dependent growth). Inflammation to scar is WHO. Nerve changes to pain that stays on is ESHRE and CMAJ (central sensitisation), marked uncertain. Lesion growth to period pain is WHO and NHS. Lesion growth to pain with sex is NICE, marked likely. Lesion growth to bowel or bladder symptoms is Lægehåndbogen and Patienthåndbogen, as deep disease involving those organs, not scar. Lesion growth to fatigue is WHO: heavy bleeding can contribute to iron deficiency and fatigue. The old edge from sensitised pain to fatigue was removed for that reason. Scar to trouble conceiving is uncertain: Patienthåndbogen says scar "kan" cause adhesions in or around the tubes. The clinical Lægehåndbogen page describes dividing adhesions at surgery, but the "kan" sentence is on the patient handbook. Growth has four outgoing edges, which is the diagram limit.

## Appointment sheet

## Urgent care

The "when to see a doctor" box stays non-urgent, matching the NHS endometriosis page ("Non-urgent advice: See a GP"). Chest symptoms are in a separate box. NHS endometriosis: endometriosis in the chest "may cause shortness of breath and coughing up blood". NHS coughing up blood: call 999 or go to A&E if you cough up more than a few spots or streaks, or if blood comes with difficulty breathing or chest pain. The Danish page does not name 112 or 1813, because those numbers were not re-opened on an official Danish page in this pass.

## Find care, Denmark, checked 28 Oct 2026

Kept only what these pages said:

- Lægehåndbogen (updated 12 Jun 2026): GP first treatment, referral to a gynaecologist, referral to a highly specialised centre for suspected deep disease.
- Lægemiddelstyrelsen, "Ryeqo bevarer generelt tilskud": general reimbursement remains for the endometriosis indication after earlier medical or surgical treatment.
- medicin.dk, Ryeqo: the same indication. No price and no dispensing group were copied, because those details were not re-read as a reimbursement fact.
- Aarhus University Hospital, Klinik for Kvindesygdomme: one of four teams treats endometriosis. No phone number was on the page, so none is shown.
- Endometriose Fællesskabet, rådgivning and the homepage: the organisation was founded on 21 Aug 1997 as Endometriose Foreningen and is now Endometriose Fællesskabet. Phone counselling is open to everyone.

Ryeqo: Lægemiddelstyrelsen, 19 Feb 2024, "Ryeqo bevarer generelt tilskud", after the indication was extended to endometriosis in women with earlier medical or surgical treatment. The card says "people treated before" as a plain-language version of that clause.

Cut from the Denmark record: Yselty reimbursement, medicine prices, and staff counts. Lægehåndbogen does say GnRH analogues should not be used beyond 6 months without add-back. That duration is not put back on the care card. The diagram uses the add-back phrase from the same paragraph.
